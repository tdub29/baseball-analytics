# Sample fixtures (committed, public-safe)

Small slices of the real data shape, committed so `dbt build` runs green on a
fresh public clone with **no credentials and no full-season dump**. The full raw
exports are gitignored (see `warehouse/.gitignore`).

| File | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `sample_trackman_pitches.csv` | ~600 | First valid pitches of the USD TrackMan master file, column-subset to what `stg_trackman__pitches` reads. The 4 known sub-50-mph mis-tracked rows are excluded so CI is clean (the velo DQ test surfaces them on the full data). |
| `sample_643_pitching.csv` | 40 | 6-4-3 per-(pitcher, pitch-type) grades for ~24 San-Diego-area pitchers (real multi-pitch arsenals, so the rollup mart is meaningful). |
| `sample_643_hitting.csv` | 30 | 6-4-3 overall hitting lines for 30 hitters. |

Trevor is fine with the **data shape** being public; this just avoids bulk-dumping
a full season into git. The coaches'-notes CRM data is never sampled or committed —
it is out of this warehouse's scope entirely.

Regenerated by slicing the real CSVs (kept out of git); not auto-generated in CI.
